Welcome to my website!

I am Alessandro Pozzato, a young person with many passions and hobbies, living in Verona, Italy, and I am 22 years old. Born as an artist and ended up becoming a developer by passion🖥️ (or maybe because I never know what to choose), I love mixing what I like to do by creating projects (or messes) and not just!

Over the years, I have developed a diverse set of skills including: game development (both digital and non-digital), musical abilities (I have also written some songs!🎶), digital art creation, and manual skills of all kinds!

When I am not programming or creating, you can find me in the mountains, as a scout leader with my wolves🐺 (they are not real wolves, they are just children I swear), playing music or looking for new things to learn, sometimes I even get to be a STEM teacher.🪫

My life is based on this digital/fisical contrast, because I switch from spending hours programming to going on mountain hikes for weeks without technology with no problems.⛰️
